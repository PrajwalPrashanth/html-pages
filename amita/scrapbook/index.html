Origin story

I’m from Indore. I started freelancing in December 2018 — coupon articles, daily deadlines, an editor’s initials in every filename.

Nobody’s showreel starts there. Mine did, and it taught me to file on time, which turns out to be half the job.

Mumbai came next: agency side, at TeamPumpkin, 2021 to 2023. Insurance, cookware, lending, steel. That’s where I learned to write for a category that doesn’t want to be written about.

Since 2023 I’ve run my own freelance practice — retainers, not gigs. Skincare, luxury, jewellery, tea, restaurants, a psychotherapist’s brochure, a diagnostic centre’s growth plan.

Now I’m at Eventually Co, and the job is bigger than copy. I write the content strategy — positioning, buckets, platform plans — and I’m on set directing the shoots that come out of it: doctors doing piece-to-camera, hotels, restaurants, founder portraits. Writing the script and then standing behind the camera while someone says it out loud changes how you write the next one.

And I’m doing a PGDM at Goa Institute of Management, in Operations and Finance. Not a career change — a modifier. I’d rather be the content person who can read the P&L than the one who leaves the room when it comes up.

Revibe Summer Sessions

Why A mixer can’t post its way into a Saturday night. It has to be the Saturday night. The brief wasn’t an ad. It was three cities, three dates, and people who had to actually show up.

BiodermaEp. 03

Seven months on retainer

SkincareFreelanceAug 2023 – Feb 2024 to confirm

Why Skincare social has two failure modes. Either it’s a product shot with a claim on it, or it’s a science lecture. Both get scrolled. The brand needed posts that taught something and still looked like the brand.

What A monthly system: December campaign ideation → client plan deck with stated objectives and tone → content buckets per product range → a dated calendar → daily copy. Then the part most people don’t show — the approval tracker.

How One post is the whole case. A carousel came back from the brand with a note: the follow-up wouldn’t land until an earlier step was covered first. They were right. I rewrote it to cover that step, and it was approved. to confirm That exchange is the job. Not the caption — the note before it.

I logged every one of them. Concept, first draft, the brand’s note, revision, approved, final caption, post timing. It isn’t a filing habit. It’s the argument: you can see what I got wrong and what I did next.

My part Wrote the concepts, the calendars and every caption. Took the notes. Made the revisions.

Result Retainer ran seven months. 172 numbered rows in the four-month tracker, Oct 2023 – Jan 2024 (44 / 27 / 50 / 51 per sheet) — this is a row count from the tracker, not a count of posts confirmed published and approved; the sheets include some blank and duplicated rows. Wealth management & a hospital fitness clubEp. 04

Strategy, not just posts

Wealth managementHospital fitness clubEventually Co2025 to confirm

Why Two clients, same request: give us posts. Neither had a reason for anyone to follow them. A calendar can’t fix that. You have to decide what the brand is for first.

What Two strategy documents. For a wealth-management firm: a positioning line, five named content buckets — each with its own job, not just a topic — a per-platform plan for LinkedIn, Instagram, podcast and video, and a full podcast format with three episode types, runtimes and a repurposing chain down to reels.

For a hospital’s fitness club: goals, seven buckets each opening with a stated objective, then the executions — post titles, carousel scripts, a lead-gen offer, geo-targeting logic — and a closing section on what the whole thing is supposed to earn the business.

How The decision in both was to lead with the thing the client thought was boring. The fitness club kept selling equipment; every gym sells equipment. It sits inside a hospital. So the line became where fitness meets medical trust, and the doctors went into the content plan, not the footer. The wealth firm kept publishing market updates; so does everyone. The bucket that earns the fee is the one where the advisor shows how he reviews a portfolio.

My part Wrote both strategy documents end to end.

Result to confirm Proxy: both documents descend from goal → bucket → objective → execution, and each bucket carries its rationale before its examples. That structure is the deliverable.

A bespoke furniture studio and its founderEp. 05

A founder’s LinkedIn, in her own voice

Bespoke furnitureEventually CoOct 2025 – Mar 2026 to confirm

Why Founders on LinkedIn tend to sound like their own brochure. The brand account can do that. The person can’t — the whole value of a founder’s post is that a brand couldn’t have written it.

What One calendar running two voices in parallel: the studio’s account and the founder’s personal one. Eleven columns — date, brand post, founder post, Instagram concept, Instagram copy, format, and the client’s remarks coming back into the same sheet, across six monthly sheets (Oct 2025 – Mar 2026). 20,331 words total across the sheet.

How The rule for the founder’s voice was that she had to admit a cost. Not a lesson — a cost. So the posts say what the work actually took: The brand account got the craft. The founder got the doubt. Two voices, one calendar, no overlap.

My part Wrote both voices. Ran the calendar. The client’s remarks are logged in the sheet, and the revisions came back through me.

Result 20,331 words across six monthly sheets (Oct 2025 – Mar 2026) — this is the sheet’s total word count, including the client’s remarks and links, not a clean count of authored copy alone — with the client’s replies inside the document, which is how the revision loop stayed in one place instead of in a chat thread. to confirm

Cookware & a lending appEp. 06

Hinglish that sells

Cookware + lendingTeamPumpkin2021–23

Why Two categories nobody brings up at a party: a pressure cooker and a loan app. Both were posting product features into a feed that only rewards recognition.

What Culture-first social for both. For the cookware brand: kitchen truths instead of kitchen shots. For the lending app, running as an IPL team’s digital partner: money worries mapped to lines people already know by heart.

How Hinglish only when the insight is actually cultural. In India we don’t ask khana khaya, we say khana kha ke jana — that sentence isn’t a translation, it’s the whole insight about how Indian homes show care. Same with Aamdani athanni phir bhi kharcha rupaiya? — a loan app can’t lecture you about money, but a phrase your family already uses can.

And the one I’d still defend in a room: on International Men’s Day, for a cookware brand — a cooker is supposed to be strong; men are allowed to be soft. Cultural insight, product truth, one line.

My part Wrote the copy inside the agency account team. Not media, not design, not the RCB partnership deal. to confirm

Result Two live posts show roughly 2.6k and 3k public likes in the captured screenshots. Provenance: public like counts visible on live posts; capture date unknown, paid support unknown. to confirm

Amita DongreProcess

How I work

1

I read before I write.

Audience, competitors, the client’s own literature, the last six months of their feed.

For skincare that meant ingredient decks. For steel it meant a plant’s vocabulary. The joke comes after the research, never instead of it.

2

I decide, then I make.

Buckets before posts. Objectives before ideas. A page framework before website copy.

If I can’t say what a piece is for in one line, it doesn’t get made.

3

I use AI, and I’ll tell you where.

It’s good for research passes, first drafts and reformatting. It is bad at the specific thing I’m paid for: knowing that khana kha ke jana is an insight and not a translation.

So the drafts can be assisted. The judgment and the joke are mine, and I say which is which.

4

I keep the feedback where you can see it.

On the skincare retainer I logged 172 numbered rows: concept, first draft, the brand’s note, the revision, the approved version. When a client said a peg wouldn’t land, that went in the sheet too.

You should be able to see me get corrected. That’s the fastest way to know how I’ll work with you.
