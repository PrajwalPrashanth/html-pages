Ep. 09 · Healthcare

A diagnostic centre’s growth plan

Healthcare · White-label, through an agency · confirm: year, and agency sign-off to describe the engagement

  • WhyA radiology clinic doesn’t grow by posting more. It grows by knowing how many people in its own town could ever walk through the door, and which of them it can actually reach.
  • WhatA business growth plan: current state and consequences, vision and mission, value proposition, target group, a market sizing carried down to the penetrated market, a four-tier competitive analysis, and a full business model canvas.
  • HowThe sizing is the argument. Start from the town’s population and cut down to the number the clinic already serves. Then the marketing question stops being what should we post and becomes which tier of competitor is the next two thousand coming from.
  • My partWrote the plan. White-label, through an agency. Not designed by me, and the clinical claims are the client’s.
  • Resultconfirm: adoption — was the plan approved, and did it run? Proxy: the document runs problem → market size → competitive set → business model, in that order. The structure is the exhibit; the deck stays private.
