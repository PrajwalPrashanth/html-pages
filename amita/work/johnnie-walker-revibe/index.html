Johnnie Walker Refreshing Mixer — Revibe Summer Sessions

Drinks · Bengaluru, Goa, Hyderabad · May–Jun 2025 · confirm: Eventually Co engagement or freelance

  • WhyA mixer can’t post its way into a Saturday night. It has to be the Saturday night. The brief wasn’t an ad. It was three cities, three dates, and people who had to actually show up.
  • WhatA 9:16 film for the Goa session. A three-beat countdown — 2 Days to Go, 1 Day to Go, Date & City Reveal. City key art for Bengaluru, Goa and Hyderabad. RSVP running through District by Zomato.
  • HowA poster tells you something exists. A countdown tells you it’s about to stop existing. So the campaign reads as a clock, not a flyer — three assets that only make sense in sequence, each one handing the reader to the RSVP. The city reveal is the payoff, not the opener. confirm: this reading is our synthesis of the asset sequence, not a sourced brief or strategy decision — no copy file for this campaign exists in the archive
