Ep. 08 · Scripts

Four languages, four scripts

Retail · White-label, through an agency · 2024 · confirm: agency and client sign-off to name the retailer

  • WhyA testimonial only works if the person on camera sounds like someone you know. Translate one script four times and everybody sounds like the same stranger.
  • WhatFive customer-testimonial scripts for a fashion retailer’s Free Shopping Week, in English, Hindi, Marathi and Gujarati. Each one carries an Agenda and a Focus line underneath, so the shoot knows what the scene is for.
  • HowDifferent protagonists, different reasons to shop. A couple starting a new year. A parent buying shoes for a son leaving for college. Someone spending a first salary on their parents. The Hindi script isn’t the English one in Hindi — it’s a two-hander, because that’s how the offer gets discussed at home.
  • My partWrote all five scripts and the Agenda and Focus line under each. White-label, through an agency.
  • Resultconfirm: whether the films were produced, and how they performed Proxy: five scripts, four languages, and the rationale shipped inside the document rather than in a covering note.

Two openings, side by side

Script 1 — English

“Hey there, it’s [Name]. So, check this out — I accidentally stumbled upon [the store]’s Free Shopping Week, and let me tell you, it’s a game-changer.”

Agenda: steal deals and discount offer.

Script 2 — Hindi

“Boyfriend: Naya saal, naye kapde! Toh hum soch rahe the ki kyun na Free Shopping Week mein kuch alag try kiya jaye.”

Agenda: a great shopping experience with free gifts.

One is a monologue to camera. The other is a two-hander, because that is how the offer actually gets discussed at home. Brand name removed from the excerpts pending sign-off.

confirm: whether the Marathi and Gujarati scripts can be excerpted too

Why hire me for this

  1. I write five different scripts, not one script translated four times.
  2. Different protagonists, different reasons to shop — the insight changes with the language.
  3. Hire me for multilingual video where each language needs its own reason to believe.
