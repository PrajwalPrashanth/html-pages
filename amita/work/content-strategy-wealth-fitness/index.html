Ep. 04 · Strategy

Strategy, not just posts

Wealth management · Hospital fitness club · Eventually Co · 2025 · confirm: client sign-off to name — until then, published anonymised

  • WhyTwo clients, same request: give us posts. Neither had a reason for anyone to follow them. A calendar can’t fix that. You have to decide what the brand is for first.
  • WhatTwo strategy documents.

    For a wealth-management firm: a positioning line, five named content buckets — each with its own job, not just a topic — a per-platform plan for LinkedIn, Instagram, podcast and video, and a full podcast format with three episode types, runtimes and a repurposing chain down to reels.

    For a hospital’s fitness club: goals, seven buckets each opening with a stated objective, then the executions — post titles, carousel scripts, a lead-gen offer, geo-targeting logic — and a closing section on what the whole thing is supposed to earn the business.
  • HowThe decision in both was to lead with the thing the client thought was boring. The fitness club kept selling equipment; every gym sells equipment. It sits inside a hospital. So the line became where fitness meets medical trust, and the doctors went into the content plan, not the footer. The wealth firm kept publishing market updates; so does everyone. The bucket that earns the fee is the one where the advisor shows how he reviews a portfolio.
  • My partWrote both strategy documents end to end.
  • Resultconfirm: adoption — was either deck approved, and did it run? Proxy: both documents descend from goal → bucket → objective → execution, and each bucket carries its rationale before its examples. That structure is the deliverable.
