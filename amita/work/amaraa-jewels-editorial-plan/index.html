Ep. 13 · Editorial planning

Plan first, then write seventeen blogs

Fine jewellery, lab-grown diamonds · Freelance · 2024

  • WhySeventeen blogs on the same category — lab-grown diamonds — repeat themselves unless someone plans the angles before anyone writes a word.
  • WhatA 20-item editorial plan, each entry carrying a one-line angle, then seventeen numbered blogs in sequence — manufacturing, the 4Cs, verification, ring sizing, corporate gifting, sustainability economics.
  • HowPlan before you write, and let the plan show its work. "Jewelry for the Modern Woman — we talk about travelling without care, flaunting jewellery without care here" is a line from the plan, not a blog itself. Seventeen blogs followed it, numbered, none repeating an angle.
  • My partWrote the 20-item plan and all seventeen numbered blogs.
  • Result17 numbered blogs, ~10,400 words, from a 20-item plan, none repeating an angle. confirm: publish dates, or any traffic or engagement figure

Numbered 1 through 17, in the order they were meant to be read. The plan is the exhibit; the volume is the proof it was followed.

Why hire me for this

  1. I plan the angles before I write, so a long series doesn't repeat itself.
  2. Seventeen numbered pieces, one plan, zero overlap.
  3. Hire me for a content series that has to hold a reader's interest past the third post.
