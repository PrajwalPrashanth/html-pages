Act 1

Every brief is a chase

A brand has a business goal it can't say out loud. An audience has a life it won't interrupt for an ad. My job is the gap in between — and every good piece of content has the same three beats.

Setup

Find the real problem

Not "post more". Insurance is boring. Cookware talks to people who don't cook. A loan app has to sound fun during the IPL. I start with the audience's actual life and the competitor set, and name the tension.

Chase

Find the cultural hook

Hinglish idiom, Bollywood dialogue, a national moment, a kitchen truth. Then a framework — pillars, a campaign spine, a calendar — so the hook survives 60 posts, not one.

Payoff

Write the line, ship the system

Copy that gets shared, briefs designers can run with, edits that keep the voice consistent, and a review loop that reads what performed and why.

Act 2

Featured episodes

Five pieces of work, told the way the work was made: setup, chase, payoff. Metrics marked confirm are being verified with clients.

Ep. 01 · Insurance

InsuranceDekho — #BharosaKarKeDekho

Campaign spine + hub content · Team Pumpkin · 2022–23

  • SetupInsurance is the product nobody wants to think about. The brand's feed was skippable and its features read like a policy document.
  • ChaseWorked within a hub-and-spoke system: one campaign spine — Bharosa kar ke dekho, "trust, but see for yourself" — with topical, USP and moment-marketing spokes. Wrote in the voice of an everyday Indian's inner monologue, not an insurer's.
  • PayoffA feed that talks like a person: reminders hidden in mirrors, a Tetris explainer for plan features, a brand that introduces itself by name.
  • “Zoom into the mirror to see a reminder.”
  • “Insurance that doesn't exist — but you want it to.”
  • “My name is Insurance. But people call me…”

Reach and engagement lift: confirm with client

Ep. 02 · Cookware

Pigeon — Kitchen truths

Social content system · Team Pumpkin · 2022

  • SetupA household cookware brand that needed to matter to twenty-five-year-olds who barely cook.
  • ChaseMoved the feed away from product shots toward Indian kitchen culture — hospitality idioms, bachelor life, gender scripts — for insight-first posts the product could sit inside.
  • PayoffA feed with opinions: a hierarchy of needs for bachelors, a Men's Day post about pressure cookers and soft men, a USP post that reads like a bedtime scene.
  • “In India we don't ask khana khaya. We say khana kha ke jana.”
  • “Cooker is supposed to be strong. Men are allowed to be soft.”

Engagement vs. product-only posts: confirm with client

Ep. 03 · Lending × IPL

KreditBee × RCB — Befikre borrowing

Brand-integration social · RCB's digital partner · IPL season

  • SetupA personal-loan app sponsoring a cricket team. Season content had to feel like the stadium, not a bank branch — in a category where tone has to stay responsible.
  • ChaseA Bollywood-dialogue framework: every money worry mapped to a line the audience already knows, then twisted toward the product. Contest and topical posts kept the cadence during matches.
  • PayoffTwo sampled posts show roughly 2.7k and 3k likes each (public counts on the live posts; paid support unknown). Which assets sat inside the RCB activation is being confirmed.
  • “Aamdani athanni, phir bhi kharcha rupaiya?”
  • “Ude dil befikre!”

Likes per post ≈ 2,676–3,024 (from screenshots) · reach: confirm

Ep. 04 · B2B

Tata Steel Industrial Consulting — Making steel readable

Digital + offline collateral · standees, brochures, emailers, webinar posts

  • SetupDeep-technical consulting — forming technologies, rolling, TQM — sold to engineers and plant heads, across trade-show standees and LinkedIn alike.
  • ChaseTranslate R&D capability into plain-English benefit ladders; one visual and verbal system across every format so a brochure and a webinar post read as the same company.
  • PayoffA webinar series, technical-meeting sponsorship creatives, and capability one-pagers that engineers could actually skim. Same writer as Ep. 02 — opposite register.

Webinar registrations / lead volume: confirm

Ep. 05 · Editorial

Team Pumpkin — Pride & Pumpkin magazine

Long-form + launch carousel · agency initiative · Pride month

Behind the scenes

How I work

The gag structure, applied to a content calendar.

Research

Audience, competitors, culture. Interviews when I can get them; comments sections when I can't.

Frame

Communication pillars, a campaign spine, creative briefs, calendars. The system that makes post 60 as sharp as post 1.

Write & edit

Lines, long-form, scripts, collateral — and the editing that keeps a voice consistent across ten hands.

Read the room

What performed, why, what to do next. AI helps with research and first drafts; the judgment and the joke are mine.
