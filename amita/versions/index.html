Six versions, one writer.

The same copy, the same eighteen episode pages, six design languages. Pick one to read. Each keeps its own address; none of them replaces the others.

  3. 03 · /amita/diner/ Diner A retro product site where the product is a writer. Oxblood and cream, ticker strips, stickers, step cards. alternate
  4. 04 · /amita/scrapbook/ Scrapbook A ring binder on a desk. Paper on paper, polaroids, tape labels, pill tags, one italic word per heading. alternate
  5. 05 · /amita/moodboard/ Moodboard A black pinboard of cut-outs with yellow handwriting. Everything floats, everything doodles when you hover. alternate · animated
  6. 06 · /amita/collage/ Collage A kraft-paper zine. Halftone cut-outs with sticker outlines, torn paper, washi tape, marker headlines. alternate
