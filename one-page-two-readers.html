That's the bit that makes it work. Your note is attached to that part — so when the agent rewrites it, the note is still sitting there on the new version. It doesn't come loose.

No call, no notetaker, no re-explaining. The note travels; the page keeps the receipt.

So the answer to your question is: nothing needs to resolve a fight between two agents, because they were never holding the same thing.

The fight worth solving is between a person and their own agent — and software can't settle that either. What it can do is stop hiding where to look.

The part I don't have an answer to yet. When something changes, it's easy to point at what to look at — you show what moved. On a brand new document, nothing points anywhere. The agent made two hundred decisions and doesn't know which three mattered. That one's still open.
